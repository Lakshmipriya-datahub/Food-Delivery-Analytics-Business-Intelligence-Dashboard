# Food-Delivery-Analytics-Business-Intelligence-Dashboard

An end-to-end Business Intelligence & Data Analytics Solution built using Qlik Sense / Qlik Cloud. This dashboard provides actionable operational insights, financial performance metrics, customer behavior trends, and delivery efficiency analyses for a large-scale food delivery business.

## Problem Statement

Food delivery platforms generate large volumes of data related to orders, customers, restaurants, food items, delivery operations, discounts, revenue and customer behavior.

However, raw transactional data makes it difficult for business teams to quickly understand:

• What are our overall order volume, and city-wise trends?

• Which customer segments place repeat orders, and how is retention changing?

• Which restaurants, cuisines, and food items perform best?

• How much revenue is being generated?

• How fast and on-time are our order deliveries across different areas?

• What are the major profitability and loss patterns?

• What operational factors cause order delays and cancellations?

• How are discounts affecting overall margins and profitability?

• Which payment methods drive the highest net revenue?

Therefore, the objective of this project is to transform raw food-delivery data into an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard using Qlik Sense, enabling stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify trends, understand customer and operational performance, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis Breakdown

The Qlik Application consists of 5 Core Sheets:

1. 📈 Executive Overview

Provides high-level strategic summaries for stakeholders.

Monthly Revenue Trend: Visualizes revenue constancy throughout the year (~₹30M - ₹35M monthly range).

Order Status Distribution: Categorizes completed deliveries (85.1%), cancellations (7.9%), returned orders, and failed deliveries.

Geographic Order Breakdown: Displays order volumes across major cities like Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, and Pune.

Revenue by Cuisine Type: Highlights top-earning cuisines including North Indian, Chinese, Fast Food, and Biryani.

2. 👥 Customer Analysis

Focuses on retention rates, lifetime value (LTV), and acquisition trends.

Customer Segmentation: Classifies users into Loyal Customers, Regular Customers, Occasional Customers, and One-Time Customers.

Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV): ₹7.97k on average.

New Customer Acquisition Trend: Tracks month-over-month new user growth (highlighting a declining acquisition rate from 2.45k in Jan to 403 in Dec).

Retention Trend: Tracks user retention rates consistently around 23% - 24% monthly.

3. 🏪 Restaurant & Food Analysis

Evaluates merchant partners and menu performance.

Total Partner Restaurants: 7,865

Orders per Restaurant: Average of 64 orders per partner.

Top Performing Restaurant: Nic Natural Ice Creams

Lowest Performing Restaurant: Food Corner

Order Distribution by Cuisine: Tracks percentage distribution (Default 32.2%, North Indian 25.1%, Chinese 17.7%, Fast Food 9.8%, South Indian 7.0%, etc.).

4. 🚚 Delivery & Operations Analysis

Monitors logistics, agent performance, and fulfillment times.

Average Daily Deliveries: 684

Total Deliveries Processed: 460.4k

Pickup vs. Delivery Time Correlation: Density scatter plot modeling logistics efficiency.

Key Delivery Rates: Cancellation Rate (7.9%), Late Delivery Rate (6.7%).

Top Delivery Agents: Identifies high-performing fleet partners based on total orders handled.

5. 💰 Profitability & Loss Analysis

Analyzes promotional spend, discount erosion, and payment methods.

Total Discounts Given: ₹22.71 Million

High-Discount Orders: 11.4% of total orders (Discount Rate: 5.4%).

Orders Share by Discount: 75.6% No Discount, 14.1% High Discount, 10.3% Low Discount.

Most Popular Payment Method: UPI

Discount-to-Revenue Ratio: Measures net revenue margin health.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

High Loyalty & Repeat Purchases: A 96.8% repeat purchase rate demonstrates high platform loyalty and user satisfaction.

Declining Acquisition: New customer growth saw a significant drop over the 12-month period. Targeted acquisition strategies are recommended.

Discount Optimization: Over ₹22.71M was allocated in discounts. Refining promotional strategies can directly protect net profit margins.

Delivery Efficiency: While On-Time Delivery is strong at 93.3%, reducing the 7.9% cancellation rate can recover significant lost revenue.
